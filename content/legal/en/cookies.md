### GSA — COOKIE POLICY AND OTHER TRACKERS

Version: 1.0.0 Last updated: [24/08/2026]

### Article 1 — Purpose

This Cookie Policy and other trackers policy aims to inform users of the GSA platform about the use of cookies and other similar technologies that may be stored or used when browsing the platform. It notably explains: - what cookies and trackers are; - why GSA uses them; - the categories of cookies that may be used; - the conditions under which the user's consent is collected; - the options available to the user to manage their preferences. This policy supplements GSA's Privacy Policy.

### Article 2 — What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small file or identifier that may be stored on the user's device when visiting a website. Cookies may notably enable: - the technical operation of the website; - the retention of certain preferences; - user authentication; - session security; - audience measurement; - analysis of website usage; - or, where applicable and authorized, certain personalization or advertising operations. Other technologies may perform functions similar to cookies. This policy also applies to these technologies when used by GSA.

### Article 3 — Strictly necessary cookies

Certain cookies are necessary for the operation of the platform. They may notably be used to: - maintain a user session; - enable authentication; - retain certain information necessary for the operation of the cart; - secure the platform; - prevent fraudulent activities; - remember cookie preferences; - ensure the technical operation of services requested by the user. These cookies may be stored without consent when they are strictly necessary for the provision of a service expressly requested by the user or for the operation of the service.

### Article 4 — Cookies subject to consent

GSA may use cookies or trackers that are not strictly necessary for the operation of the platform. Depending on the tools actually used, this may notably concern: - audience measurement; - browsing analysis; - personalization; - certain features offered by third-party services; - advertising or advertising tracking. Where their use requires the user's consent, these cookies are only stored after obtaining that consent.

### Article 5 — Managing preferences

Where consent is required, the user can choose the categories of cookies they accept or refuse using the preference management mechanism provided on the platform. The user can change their choices at any time. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before that withdrawal.

### Article 6 — List of cookies used

The list below reflects the cookies and trackers actually used by GSA at the time of publication of this policy:

| Cookie / tracker | Provider | Purpose | Duration | Consent |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `connect.sid` | GSA | User login session and authentication | 30 days | No (strictly necessary) |
| `gsa-locale` | GSA | Remembers the preferred language | Persistent (localStorage) | No (user preference) |

GSA uses no advertising cookies and no third-party tracking cookies (no advertising audience measurement, no retargeting). The only cookies stored are those necessary for the operation of the platform.

### Article 7 — Cookies related to third-party services

Certain GSA features may use services provided by third parties. These services may store their own cookies or trackers. The providers concerned may notably include: - payment provider; - hosting provider; - audience measurement tool; - security tool; - services integrated into the platform. Where the use of these trackers requires the user's consent, it is collected before they are stored. The processing carried out directly by these providers may also be subject to their own privacy policies.

### Article 8 — Payment cookies

GSA uses Stripe for payment processing. Certain technologies required for payment processing or transaction security may be used by Stripe. These technologies may be subject to Stripe's policies and privacy terms. Cookies or trackers strictly necessary for the performance of an operation requested by the user may be used without consent where applicable regulations allow it.

### Article 9 — Browser settings

The user can also configure their browser to control or block certain cookies. How cookies are managed depends on the browser used. Blocking certain strictly necessary cookies may however make certain GSA features unavailable or malfunction.

### Article 10 — Retention period for choices

When the user makes a choice regarding cookies, GSA retains this choice for the necessary period in order not to ask for their consent again on every visit. The retention period for the choice is determined in accordance with applicable regulatory requirements and the settings of the consent management mechanism used by GSA.

### Article 11 — Changes to this policy

GSA may amend this Cookie Policy to take account of: - changes to the platform; - the addition or removal of certain services; - changes to the technologies used; - changes to applicable regulations. The applicable version is the one published on the platform at the time of consultation. Where necessary, GSA may ask the user for their consent again.
