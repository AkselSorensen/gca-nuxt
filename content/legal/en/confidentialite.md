GSA processes personal data for the following main purposes: - account creation and management; - processing of orders and delivery of digital content; - management of payments; - seller relationship management; - provision of customer support; - security of the platform and fraud prevention; - improvement of services; - compliance with legal obligations. The details of each processing are set out below where applicable.

### Article 5 — Legal bases for processing

GSA processes personal data on the basis of the legal grounds applicable to each processing operation. These bases may notably be: Contract performance Where processing is necessary to provide a service requested by the user, notably: - creation and management of an account; - purchase of content; - order processing; - management of a seller relationship; - provision of contractually agreed support. Legal obligation Where GSA must process or retain certain data to comply with a legal obligation. Legitimate interest Where the processing is necessary for GSA's legitimate interests, notably to: - ensure the security of the platform; - prevent fraud; - defend its rights; - improve its services; - ensure the technical management of the website. GSA ensures in this case that the rights and freedoms of the data subjects are preserved. Consent Where consent is legally required, notably for certain cookies, trackers or electronic communications, it is collected under the applicable conditions. Consent may be withdrawn under the conditions provided by the regulations.

Personal data may be accessible, within the limits of their respective needs, to: - GSA; - its technical service providers; - its payment provider; - its hosting providers; - its IT service providers; - its support providers; - its professional advisers where necessary; - the competent authorities where required by law. Some technical providers used by GSA may process personal data from a country located outside the European Union or the European Economic Area. The transfers may notably be based, depending on the provider and the processing concerned, on: - an adequacy decision adopted by the European Commission; - standard contractual clauses adopted by the European Commission; - or any other appropriate safeguard provided for by the GDPR. Depending on the processing concerned and the configuration of the services used, Stripe may transfer data to countries outside the European Union or the European Economic Area. Stripe states that it uses, where necessary, various transfer mechanisms compliant with applicable requirements, notably the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. Information about Stripe's data processing is available in its privacy policy and its data transfer documentation. Other providers GSA may also use other technical providers, notably for hosting, security, maintenance, sending communications or operating the platform. The list and applicable processing arrangements will be updated where necessary to reflect the providers actually used by GSA.
