### GSA — WITHDRAWAL, REFUND AND COMPLAINTS POLICY

Version: 1.0.0 Last updated: [24/08/2026]

### Article 1 — Purpose

This policy defines the conditions applicable to withdrawal, refund and problem resolution requests that may arise following a purchase made on the GSA platform. It notably applies to: - purchases of digital content; - services offered by GSA; - complaints concerning purchased content; - technical or functional problems; - requests for amicable resolution. It supplements the Terms of Sale, the GSA Standard License and, where applicable, the specific terms of the product concerned.

### Article 2 — Status of the buyer

The applicable rights may vary depending on whether the buyer acts: - as a consumer; - as a professional. Mandatory provisions of applicable law prevail over these terms. Where a legal provision grants a right to the buyer, no provision of this policy may remove or limit it contrary to the law.

### Article 3 — Consumers' right of withdrawal

Where applicable, the consumer has a period of fourteen days to exercise their right of withdrawal in respect of a distance contract. For a service, this period generally runs from the conclusion of the contract. Exercising the right of withdrawal does not require any particular justification.

### Article 4 — Digital content supplied without a tangible medium

The digital content offered on GSA may be delivered immediately after purchase. Where a consumer requests access to the digital content before the expiry of the legal withdrawal period, GSA collects, where necessary: 1. their express consent to the immediate performance of the contract; 2. their acknowledgement that they will lose their right of withdrawal when the legal conditions are met. For digital content without a tangible medium, the right of withdrawal may be excluded where the conditions provided by Article L.221-28, 13 of the French Consumer Code are met. GSA must also provide the consumer with the confirmation required by the regulations.

### Article 5 — Immediate download or access to content

Where access to or download of digital content begins immediately after purchase, the buyer is informed, before the conclusion of the contract, of the consequences of this immediate access on their possible right of withdrawal. Where the legal conditions allowing the exclusion of the right of withdrawal are met, immediate access to the content may result in the loss of that right. This exclusion only applies where the legal conditions are actually met.

### Article 6 — Services

GSA may offer services, notably in the fields of Game Design and, in the future, 3D modeling. Where a consumer expressly requests that the performance of a service begins before the expiry of the withdrawal period, GSA collects this request under the conditions provided by the regulations. If the consumer then exercises their right of withdrawal after the service has started, they may be required to pay the amount corresponding to the service provided up to the communication of their decision to withdraw, where the legal conditions are met. Where the service has been fully performed before the end of the withdrawal period, at the express request of the consumer and with acknowledgement of the loss of their right of withdrawal, the right of withdrawal can no longer be exercised in accordance with the conditions provided by law.

### Article 7 — How to exercise the right of withdrawal

Where the right of withdrawal applies, the buyer may exercise it by contacting GSA at the following address: Email: support-gsa@gsa-store.fr The request must allow GSA to identify: - the buyer; - the order concerned; - the content or service concerned; - the clear intention to exercise the right of withdrawal. The buyer may also use the withdrawal form provided by GSA where such a form is offered.

### Article 8 — Refund in case of withdrawal

Where a valid withdrawal gives rise to a refund, it is made in accordance with the applicable legal rules. Unless otherwise applicable, the refund is made using the same payment method as the one used for the transaction. GSA may use the payment provider used for the transaction to make the refund.

### Article 9 — No automatic refund after delivery of digital content

Where digital content was delivered immediately after purchase and the legal conditions for excluding the right of withdrawal were validly met, the buyer cannot obtain a refund by simply invoking the right of withdrawal. This provision does not deprive the buyer of the rights they enjoy regarding conformity, defective content, content not matching the description or any other applicable legal guarantee.

### Article 10 — Defective or non-compliant content

Where purchased content presents a major problem, the buyer may contact GSA to report the problem. This may notably concern: - missing file; - corrupted file; - unusable content; - content substantially different from its description; - a problem preventing its normal use; - the presence of malware; - a security problem; - a major defect affecting the functioning of the content. GSA analyzes the situation and may, depending on the case: - provide the missing files; - provide a corrected version; - help resolve the problem; - contact the seller; - ask the seller to correct the content; - temporarily suspend the content; - remove the content from the platform; - propose an amicable solution; - carry out or have carried out a refund where applicable or agreed.

### Article 11 — GSA support

GSA provides support for purchases made on the platform. Unless a specific provision applies to the content, after-sales support is provided for a period of seven days from the purchase. GSA support may notably handle: - access problems; - missing files; - download-related problems; - obvious errors in the content provided; - difficulties relating to the order. Where the problem directly relates to the design or functioning of the content, GSA may put the buyer in contact with the seller.

### Article 12 — Role of the seller in resolving a problem

Where a problem relates to the design, functioning or content itself of a product, the seller remains responsible for the elements relating to their creation and their contractual obligations towards GSA. GSA may act as an intermediary to facilitate the resolution of the problem between the buyer and the seller. The fact that GSA provides first-level support does not mean that GSA becomes the author or technical party responsible for the content created by the seller.

### Article 13 — Amicable solution

In case of a major problem, GSA may propose an amicable resolution between the buyer and the seller. Depending on the circumstances, this resolution may notably take the form of: - a correction of the content; - the provision of a new version; - a replacement of the file; - technical assistance; - a credit note; - a refund; - any other solution agreed between the parties. An amicable solution may be proposed even where no automatic refund is provided by the product's commercial terms, subject to the applicable mandatory rights.

### Article 14 — Security or malware cases

Where content is suspected of containing malware or presenting a security risk, GSA may immediately suspend its access or distribution pending verification. The buyer is invited to report any suspicious behavior to GSA without delay. After analysis, GSA may notably: - suspend the content; - temporarily or permanently remove the content; - contact the seller; - request a corrected version; - inform the users concerned; - implement an appropriate solution depending on the situation.

### Article 15 — Products removed from GSA

The subsequent removal of content from the platform does not automatically deprive the buyer of the rights they validly acquired before that removal. However, where content must be removed for legal, security or intellectual property reasons, GSA may take the measures necessary to protect users and comply with its obligations. The consequences of such a removal are assessed according to the situation concerned and the applicable rights.

### Article 16 — Chargebacks and disputes with the payment provider

The buyer is invited to contact GSA before initiating a dispute procedure with their bank or the payment provider when the problem can be resolved directly by support. This amicable approach does not prevent the buyer from exercising the rights granted to them by law. Payment disputes may lead to a verification of the transaction and of the information necessary to defend GSA's interests.

### Article 17 — Abusive requests

GSA reserves the right to review repeated or manifestly abusive requests. This provision does not prevent the exercise of the buyer's legal rights.

### Article 18 — Exclusions

No refund can be guaranteed for mere subjective dissatisfaction with content when: - the content matches its description; - it works in accordance with its advertised features; - the right of withdrawal was validly lost in accordance with the applicable provisions; - no other legal right to a refund applies. This provision does not limit the legal guarantees to which the buyer may be entitled.

### Article 19 — Professionals

The provisions relating to consumers' legal right of withdrawal do not automatically apply to professional buyers. The conditions applicable to professionals are those provided by the Terms of Sale, the contract concluded between the parties and the legal provisions applicable to their situation.

### Article 20 — Changes to this policy

GSA may amend this policy to take account of: - changes to its services; - changes to its payment arrangements; - changes to the regulations; - changes to its commercial terms. The applicable version is the one published on the platform at the time concerned.

### Article 21 — Contact

For any request concerning a withdrawal, a refund or a problem related to a purchase: GSA Email: support-gsa@gsa-store.fr The buyer is invited to provide: - the email address used at the time of purchase; - the order number; - the content concerned; - the nature of the problem; - any useful supporting documents.

### Article 22 — Entry into force

This Withdrawal, Refund and Complaints Policy enters into force on: [24/08/2026] Version: 1.0.0
